# Fitness Tracking Full Plan

Purpose: Unify BMI monitoring, wearable sync, and gamified progress.

Features:
- Real-time activity logs from Pixel/Coros/Fitbit
- Goal setting + diet plan tracking
- Barcode scanning for keto classification
- Cross-platform (Android, watch, laptop)
- Push reminders for missed workouts

Implementation:
1. Pull metrics from Fitbit/Pixel Watch.
2. Convert swim laps → step equivalents.
3. Log to memory/logs/fitness/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
4. Sync summaries weekly to Google Sheets.
